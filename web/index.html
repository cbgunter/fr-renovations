Our work

The jobs we do most.

Most of our work falls into six kinds of jobs. If yours isn't here, ask. If it's a fit, we'll say so; if it isn't, we'll tell you who to call.

Decks & porches

New decks, rebuilds and repairs in pressure-treated, cedar or composite, with stairs and railings to code.

Kitchen remodels

Cabinets, countertops, tile and flooring, with the plumbing and electrical done by licensed trades.

Bathroom remodels

Half baths to full primary baths. Tub-to-shower conversions, new vanities, and tile that's waterproofed underneath.

Roofing

Full tear-offs and re-roofs. We replace soft decking, flash every wall and chimney, and haul everything away.

Siding & windows

Vinyl, fiber cement or wood siding, and replacement windows set, flashed and sealed properly.

Basement finishing

Framing, drywall, bathrooms and egress windows, so the space is usable and there's a safe way out.
