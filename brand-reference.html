05 · Keep it honest

What we don't do.

No fire-department names, patches, badges, flames or sirens.

No gradients, glows, heavy shadows or colored left-border cards.

No plaid backgrounds — a 10px band or a small mark only.

No invented reviews, stats or years in business. Use [placeholders].

No status shown by color alone. Always a word.

No stock "smiling contractor" photos. Real jobs only.
